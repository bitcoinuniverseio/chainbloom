# Plain-language legal context

ChainBloom is a creative coordination system that uses ordinary Bitcoin
transactions. It does not create a project token, account balance, financial
claim, ownership registry, royalty right, public mint, or official trading
market.

Control of a ChainBloom carrier means control of a specific Bitcoin output. It
does not by itself prove authorship, copyright ownership, identity, permission
to reuse media, or legal title to an artwork.

Creative material, events, communities, and hosted services can have their own
terms. A creator should have permission to share the material they contribute.
An organization should describe its own privacy, moderation, refund, access,
and content policies in clear language suited to its audience and location.

Bitcoin transaction fees may apply. A confirmed action cannot be reversed by
ChainBloom. Tax, consumer, intellectual-property, accessibility, privacy, and
financial rules can differ by place and by activity.

This page explains the product in ordinary language and is not legal advice.
People and organizations should seek qualified advice for their own facts.
