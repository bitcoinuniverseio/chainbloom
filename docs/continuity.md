# Product continuity

> This page has moved. Read
> [Version, changes and what is next](https://bitcoinuniverseio.github.io/chainbloom/docs/reference/changelog/)
> for the current version.

ChainBloom is designed for worlds that remain understandable over time.

Confirmed history stays anchored to Bitcoin rather than one website. Public
guides explain the experience in stable language. Compatible services can
reconstruct worlds independently, and galleries can change their presentation
without changing the shared sequence.

When the product evolves, people should receive a clear explanation of what is
changing, why it improves the experience, and whether any action is needed.
Existing confirmed worlds should remain viewable and their meaning should not
be silently redefined.

Security fixes may require urgent action, but communication should still be
plain, factual, and focused on what people need to know. Historical notices
should remain available so communities can understand how the product has
grown.
