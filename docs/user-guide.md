# Take part in ChainBloom

> This page has moved. Read
> [Joining a world](https://bitcoinuniverseio.github.io/chainbloom/docs/participate/join-a-world/)
> for the current version, which covers fees, review, and confirmation in detail.

[Open ChainBloom](https://inscribe.bitcoinuniverse.io/chainbloom) to find a
world or continue a path.

## Find a world

Choose a world whose theme and community feel meaningful to you. Read its
creative prompt, participation window, and community expectations before you
begin.

## Choose your contribution

You may add a bloom, echo an earlier moment, connect your path with another, or
complete a path when its story feels finished. A preview shows how the choice
can appear before anything is signed.

## Review the Bitcoin transaction

Your wallet shows the world, path, action, destination, amount, and network fee.
Read the complete review. If the action is unexpected, the fee feels wrong, or
the wallet cannot clearly identify the ChainBloom path, stop before signing.

## Wait for confirmation

An unconfirmed contribution is provisional and may change or disappear. Once
Bitcoin confirms it, compatible ChainBloom views place it in the shared
history.

## Return to the story

Keep the transaction identifier of your last step and the outpoint of your live
path, then check them on a Bitcoin explorer whenever you want. A meeting does
not erase either path, and a completed path stays part of the world's history.

## Keep control safely

Protect your seed phrase and private keys. Never share them with a gallery,
community host, or support account.

A live path is one specific small Bitcoin output. If an ordinary payment spends
it, the path ends and nothing can bring it back. No released wallet recognises
these outputs yet, so keep a live path in an account you do not spend from and
write down its outpoint.

ChainBloom participation is creative, not an investment. There is no project
token, reward, royalty, public mint, or official trading market.
