ChainBloom combines creative choices with real Bitcoin transactions. A few
simple habits keep participation clear and calm.

## Review before signing

Read the complete wallet review, including the action, world, path, destination,
amount, and network fee. If anything is unfamiliar or different from what you
intended, stop before signing.

## Protect the path

A live ChainBloom path is carried by a specific Bitcoin output. Use a wallet
that recognizes it and keeps it out of ordinary spending. Spending it outside
the intended ChainBloom action can end that path permanently.

## Keep keys private

No gallery, community host, or support person needs your seed phrase or private
key. Store recovery information securely and treat unexpected signing requests
as suspicious.

## Know what is confirmed

Activity awaiting confirmation is a preview. It can be replaced or disappear.
Confirmed history follows Bitcoin, including any change caused by a recent
chain reorganization.

## Verify important moments

For a contribution that matters deeply, keep the transaction identifier and
compare it with a trusted Bitcoin explorer. A screenshot, animation, profile
badge, or social post is not proof by itself.

## Understand the limits

ChainBloom cannot reverse a confirmed transaction, recover lost keys, promise
continuous access to a hosted gallery, or establish legal authorship and
ownership by itself. These limits are part of honest participation, not reasons
to make the experience less ambitious.

If you discover a security problem, use the
[private reporting path](./vulnerability-disclosure.md).
