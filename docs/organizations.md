> guidance on moderation, privacy, and access.

ChainBloom gives organizations a distinctive way to invite participation
without turning community creativity into a points program or financial
product.

## Experiences that fit naturally

### Cultural exhibitions

Let visitors shape a bounded work across the life of an exhibition, then keep
the resulting history available as a public archive.

### Festivals and live programs

Connect stages, neighborhoods, or days through distinct paths that meet at
shared moments.

### Learning and public education

Make confirmed ordering, shared state, and collaborative authorship visible
through an experience people can understand by taking part.

### Membership and community rituals

Create recurring acts of contribution that reflect a community's culture
without promising a reward, yield, or resale value.

### Artist and brand collaborations

Give an artist a clear creative frame and let the audience add meaning over
time. Sponsorship can support the experience while the shared history remains
open and independently viewable.

## Sustainable value

Organizations can fund curation, production, accessible presentation,
education, hosting, and support. These are services around ChainBloom, not fees
or financial rights created by ChainBloom itself.

Strong programs set a clear creative purpose, explain Bitcoin transaction fees
before participation, protect privacy, and measure success through meaningful
engagement rather than trading activity.
