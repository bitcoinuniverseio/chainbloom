# Transparency and user expectations

ChainBloom is built around a simple promise: the shared creative history should
be clear, independently observable, and separate from marketing claims.

## What ChainBloom provides

- a bounded world with distinct creative paths;
- a confirmed public ordering carried by Bitcoin;
- clear ways to begin, contribute, connect paths, and complete them;
- consistent histories across compatible services; and
- freedom for galleries to interpret the history creatively.

## What ChainBloom does not create

ChainBloom has no project token, balance, reward, royalty, public mint, price,
sale, listing, transfer market, or official marketplace. A third-party service
may offer separate products under its own identity and terms, but those
products are not ChainBloom actions.

## Confirmation and previews

Unconfirmed activity is provisional. It may be replaced, conflict, disappear,
or change order. Confirmed history follows Bitcoin. A recent chain
reorganization can change the newest confirmed sequence, and trustworthy views
update to match it.

## Keys and identity

Wallet control authorizes a Bitcoin spend. It does not automatically establish
a person's real-world identity, authorship, copyright, or legal ownership.
Profiles and social labels add context but remain separate from the confirmed
history.

## Hosted experiences

Websites, galleries, notifications, moderation, and media hosting can be
temporarily unavailable. A service should communicate interruptions plainly
and should never present a preview as confirmed.

## Honest communication

Claims about adoption, partnerships, audits, performance, permanence, or legal
status need current evidence. ChainBloom's strongest story is already exciting:
people can create a shared, confirmed history without a speculative layer.
