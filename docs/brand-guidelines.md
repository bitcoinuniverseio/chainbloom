# Brand and community expression

ChainBloom should feel alive, precise, welcoming, and unmistakably grounded in
Bitcoin without borrowing the visual language of trading products.

## The central idea

Show distinct paths that grow, meet, and continue. A meeting should feel like a
shared moment rather than a merger or sale. The strongest compositions combine
open space, a clear rhythm, and one memorable bloom.

## Voice

Use direct, warm language:

- “Grow a shared history.”
- “Let two paths meet.”
- “Return to the world you helped shape.”
- “Review the complete Bitcoin transaction before you sign.”

Avoid price language, scarcity hype, ownership promises, or claims that a
visual image proves a confirmed event.

## Color and type

- **Deep Ink `#07111F`** gives the experience depth.
- **Warm Paper `#F3F0E8`** keeps long-form reading comfortable.
- **Living Mint `#7EE0BD`** marks growth and safe action.
- **Bloom Coral `#FF8B70`** marks human contribution.
- **Sun `#F4CC62`** highlights meetings and attention.

Editorial serif headings bring warmth. Clear sans-serif body text keeps the
experience approachable. Transaction details should remain easy to read and
copy wherever they appear.

## Official assets

- [ChainBloom logo](../site/assets/chainbloom-logo.svg)
- [ChainBloom mark](../site/assets/chainbloom-mark.svg)
- [ChainBloom social card](../site/assets/chainbloom-og.png)

Keep their proportions intact and preserve generous clear space. Meaningful
images need useful alt text; motion needs a still alternative and should honor
reduced-motion preferences.

Community art can extend the garden metaphor in any medium. Label community
work clearly, credit its creator, and do not imply an endorsement that has not
been given.
