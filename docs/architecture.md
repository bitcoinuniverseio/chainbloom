# How the ChainBloom experience comes together

ChainBloom feels simple because each part has a clear job.

## Creation

InScribe turns an idea into a guided flow. A creator shapes a world, previews
the paths, invites participation, and sees the complete Bitcoin transaction
before signing.

## Control

Universe Wallet recognizes the special Bitcoin outputs that carry a
ChainBloom path. It keeps them away from ordinary coin selection, explains the
action being signed, and helps participants return to worlds they care about.

## Shared history

Bitcoin confirms and orders contributions. ChainBloom reads that public order
and reconstructs each world consistently, including meetings, completed paths,
and changes caused by a chain reorganization.

## Discovery

Universe Explorer presents worlds as histories, relationships, and visual
journeys. Profiles, bookmarks, watchlists, and notifications make it easy to
follow people and places without changing the shared record.

## Creative interpretation

The confirmed history is common; the presentation is not locked down. One
gallery may show a botanical animation, another a soundscape, and another a
quiet timeline. Different views can coexist while telling the same story.

This separation gives ChainBloom both reliability and creative freedom.
