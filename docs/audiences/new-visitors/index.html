If you are new here

For youLast reviewed 2026-07-313 min read

The ninety second version of ChainBloom, the one thing worth looking at first, and clear permission to stop reading afterwards.

You need no wallet, no account, and no bitcoin to finish this page. In about ninety seconds you will know what ChainBloom is, whether it is for you, and exactly where you are allowed to stop.

The ninety second version#

A group of people agree to grow one story together, in a space that has an ending.

Someone opens a . Opening it fixes three things forever: how many it holds (between 1 and 8), how long it stays open (between 144 and 52,560 Bitcoin blocks, roughly 1 to 365 days), and how many steps any one path may take (up to 512).

Then people take those paths forward. Each is one small Bitcoin transaction. A path can add a moment, answer an earlier moment, meet another path so that both carry on, or end on purpose.

Bitcoin is here for one reason. Everyone needs to agree on what came first, and nobody should have to host the answer. That is all it does here. There is no token, no balance, no mint, and nothing to buy.

What to look at first#

One thing, then stop.

Watch a world grow takes about two minutes. Three paths open, moments appear on them, one path answers another, two paths meet, one path ends. You will understand more from watching that once than from three pages of description, because the shape is the idea.

If you still want more after it, read one worked example. The gentlest is a collaborative garden. It shows an invitation, the paths people took, the moment they met, and how it finished.

Ignore the reference section entirely. It is written for people building software and it will make this sound harder than it is.

Permission to stop#

Here is the part most sites will not tell you.

If the ninety second version did not land, nothing further down will rescue it. ChainBloom is a narrow idea done carefully, not something everyone needs. Closing this tab is a reasonable outcome and costs you nothing.

Two honest things before you decide.

There is nothing live to browse today. The public that would let anyone look at confirmed worlds is not switched on. So you cannot go and find a world that strangers are filling in right now. What is running says precisely what does and does not work.

Taking part costs real money. Not much, but real: a Bitcoin network fee paid to miners, plus 1,000 satoshis held in the output that carries your path while it is alive. That amount is released back to you when you finish the path. Reading costs nothing and always will.

◆Note

A confirmed step cannot be edited or removed by anyone, including the person who created the world and including us. That is the point of the design, and it is also the main reason to think before your first one.

Before you go further#

  • I can say what a world is in one sentence
  • I know that the number of paths, the lifetime, and the step limit are fixed when a world is created
  • I have watched a world grow, or read one example world
  • I know that taking part means signing a real Bitcoin transaction and paying a real miner fee
  • I know that the public index is not switched on yet, so there is nothing live to browse

If you ticked all five and still want in, the next page is if you want to take part. It covers what you need, what you keep, and what you cannot undo.
