ChainBloom documentation

DocumentationLast reviewed 2026-07-312 min read

ChainBloom lets a group of people grow one shared story, one confirmed Bitcoin moment at a time. Start where you are.

Most things you make online arrive, get seen, and sink. ChainBloom gives a moment a place in a story instead: a world with a beginning, a fixed number of paths, and an ending everyone can see coming. Bitcoin puts every contribution in one order that nobody can quietly rearrange.

You do not need to understand Bitcoin to take part, and you do not need permission to start. Pick the door that matches what you want to do.

Six routes through this documentation: explore a world, join one, create one, build an integration, run infrastructure, or host a public programme. Each one is a short ordered reading list. Every page is also reachable from the menu.

What ChainBloom is not#

ChainBloom has no project token, no reward, no royalty, no public mint, no price, and no official marketplace. There is nothing to buy from us and nothing to sell. The that hold a path in place are carried forward, not spent, and they come back when the path is completed.

This matters because it changes what the work is for. A world is worth making because the story is worth having, not because the story might be worth something later.
