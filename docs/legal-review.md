# Legal review checklist

This checklist identifies questions for counsel; it is not legal advice and no
answer is implied.

- Product: Is the service software-only, hosted, custodial, creative, a contest,
  or a combination? Who is the contracting party?
- Assets and funds: Who holds Bitcoin keys or fee funds? Can the operator move,
  freeze, recover, or pool them? Are customer assets segregated?
- Marketing: Could token, ownership, rarity, reward, yield, investment, resale,
  or permanence claims mislead a reasonable user?
- Financial regulation: Do custody, transmission, sanctions, AML, securities,
  commodities, gambling, or tax rules apply in target jurisdictions?
- Consumers: Are fees, irreversible loss, expiry, reorgs, service availability,
  refunds, age limits, and complaint paths clearly disclosed?
- Privacy: What public-chain data and service telemetry are linked? What lawful
  basis, retention period, deletion limit, and cross-border transfer applies?
- Content and IP: Who licenses titles, artwork, trademarks, and renderer assets?
  How are notices, moderation, and disputes handled?
- Open source: Are MIT notices retained and dependency licenses reviewed?
- Security: Are key management, vulnerability response, backups, access logs,
  vendors, and breach notification documented?
- Geography: Are restricted regions blocked consistently and explained?

Review the exact UI, transaction flow, terms, privacy notice, support language,
and incident plan—not only the protocol specification. Repeat review after any
custody, prize, marketplace, identity, analytics, or jurisdiction change.
