ChainBloom is designed so the shared history does not depend on one gallery or
one company. Bitcoin provides the confirmed order, and multiple services can
present the same worlds independently.

## What people can expect

- Confirmed contributions appear in a stable shared sequence.
- Unconfirmed activity is labeled as a preview.
- A temporary service interruption does not rewrite confirmed history.
- If Bitcoin reorganizes recent blocks, ChainBloom views follow the resulting
  confirmed order.
- Different galleries may look different while agreeing on the underlying
  events.

## Choosing a service

Look for clear confirmation labels, recent update information, accessible
transaction links, and honest notices during an interruption. For an important
decision, compare the event with a trusted Bitcoin explorer and keep your own
transaction record.

Reliability is a shared practice: transparent services, careful wallets, and
participants who review before signing all contribute to a better experience.
