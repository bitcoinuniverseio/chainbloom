# ChainBloom public story and media kit

## Short description

ChainBloom is a shared creative experience carried by Bitcoin. People grow
distinct paths, create meaningful meetings, and leave a confirmed history that
artists and communities can interpret in many ways.

## Full description

ChainBloom turns a sequence of confirmed Bitcoin actions into a bounded
collaborative world. Each path remains distinct, so participation stays easy to
follow even when stories connect. Bitcoin provides a common order, and the same
history can be presented as a drawing, a score, a timeline, or a plain list
without any of them being less true.

The project does not create a token, reward scheme, royalty engine, public
mint, price mechanic, or official trading market. Its value is the experience:
creative participation, clear provenance, shared memory, and open artistic
interpretation.

## Key facts

- The protocol is written, tested, and published with interoperability vectors.
  A world can be created and added to today; a public index, wallet support for
  path outputs, and a browsing surface are not built yet. See
  [what is running today](https://bitcoinuniverseio.github.io/chainbloom/docs/help/status/).
- A world has a bounded arc and distinct participant paths.
- Confirmed Bitcoin transactions provide the shared ordering.
- Two paths can meet without being merged or traded.
- Galleries can create different visual or audio interpretations of the same
  history.
- Participants retain wallet control and review before signing.

## Suggested announcement

> ChainBloom is a way to grow shared creative histories with Bitcoin. Open a
> world, add your bloom, let paths meet, and come back to the story you helped
> shape. No project token and no trading market. Just collaborative creation,
> confirmed moments, and an open canvas for artists and communities.

## Interview themes

Good conversations focus on why bounded worlds feel more meaningful than an
endless feed, how distinct paths keep collaboration legible, and why separating
shared history from visual interpretation creates room for culture.

When discussing safety, explain that participation uses real Bitcoin
transactions, network fees may apply, confirmations cannot be undone, and
people should review the complete transaction in a wallet they trust.
