## The shortest way through

Read them in order. Each one leans on the last, and the whole run is shorter than it looks.

If you only have ten minutes, read **How a world grows** and the page on the five actions. That pair is enough to design a world on paper and to follow somebody else's.

If you are about to take a step for real, add **Confirmed and unconfirmed** before you sign anything. It explains why waiting is not a delay but the thing you are paying for.

## What you do not need yet

You do not need to understand Bitcoin outputs, transaction structure, or how a [[marker]] is encoded to read this section. Those live in the [technical reference](/docs/reference), and nothing here depends on them.

You also do not need money. Everything in these six pages can be read, understood, and argued with before you ever open a wallet. When you do want to take part, [take part](/docs/participate) picks up where this section stops.
