## What taking part actually involves

Joining a [[world]] means signing one Bitcoin transaction. There is nothing to buy, no account to open, and no subscription.

You pay two things and no more. A network fee to miners, the same as any other Bitcoin transaction. And {{CARRIER_VALUE_SATS}} satoshis held inside each [[path]] output for as long as that path is alive, carried from step to step and released back to you when the path is completed.

Everything else is craft. Choosing the shape of a world. Writing an invitation people want to answer. Deciding what your one moment adds. Knowing when to stop.

:::warning
A [[confirmation|confirmed]] action cannot be undone. Not by the creator, not by us, not by you. The fee is paid whether or not you liked the result. Read the review screen every time, including the tenth time.
:::

## Where to begin

If you have never done this, read [Joining and contributing to a world](/docs/participate/join-a-world) first, then [Fees and confirmation](/docs/participate/fees-and-confirmation). Together they take about fifteen minutes and they cover the two things people regret not knowing.

If you are opening a world for other people, read [Creating a world from start to finish](/docs/participate/create-a-world). Several of its settings are fixed at the moment of creation and cannot be edited afterwards, so it is worth an hour of thought before it is worth a transaction.

:::note
The public index that lets anyone browse confirmed worlds is not switched on yet, so there is no list of open worlds to scroll through today. [What is running](/docs/help/status) says exactly where things stand and what changes when it turns on.
:::
