## The five minute version

A **world** is a bounded shared story. Whoever creates it decides how many [[path|paths]] it has, how long it stays open, and how many steps each path may take. Those choices are fixed at the moment the world is created and can never be edited.

A **path** is one thread inside that world. Each path is held in place by a single small Bitcoin output. To add to a path you spend that output and create the next one. That is the whole trick: because Bitcoin will only let an output be spent once, the order of a path cannot be argued with.

There are five things anyone can do:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Create | Opens a world and its paths |
| Bloom | Adds one creative moment to a path |
| Echo | Adds a moment that points back at an earlier one |
| Meet | Lets two paths share a moment and both carry on |
| Complete | Ends a path on purpose |

That is the entire vocabulary. Everything a world becomes is made from those five moves.

## What it costs

Two things, and no more:

1. A Bitcoin network fee, paid to miners, the same as any other Bitcoin transaction. ChainBloom takes nothing.
2. {{CARRIER_VALUE_SATS}} satoshis held in each path output — carried forward from step to step, and released back to you when the path is completed.

There is no price to join, no subscription, and nothing to buy.

:::warning
Taking part means signing a real Bitcoin transaction. Read what your wallet shows you before you sign. A confirmed action cannot be undone by anyone, including us.
:::

## Where you are reading matters less than what you want

:::grid
**If you have five minutes**, read [ChainBloom in 60 seconds](/docs/start/chainbloom-in-60-seconds) and stop. It is enough to decide whether this is for you.

**If you have half an hour**, add [How a world grows](/docs/learn/how-a-world-grows) and one [example world](/docs/examples). You will be able to describe ChainBloom to someone else.

**If you are about to spend money**, read [Fees and confirmation](/docs/participate/fees-and-confirmation) and [Protect your path](/docs/participate/protect-your-path) first. Both are short.

**If you are writing code**, skip everything above and go to [Protocol architecture](/docs/reference/protocol-architecture).
:::
