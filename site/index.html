What

A world with edges

Someone opens a world. It gets a few paths, a step limit, and a final block. Those numbers are fixed the second it exists and nobody can edit them, not even the person who chose them.

How

You hold a thread

Each path is held by one small Bitcoin output. Adding your moment spends it and creates the next one. That is why the order of a path can never be argued with, rewritten, or quietly reordered later.

Why

It stays yours

Two paths can meet and both carry on. Nothing merges, nothing is absorbed, and nothing you made gets flattened into one house style.

Questions worth asking

Is ChainBloom a token or trading market?

No. ChainBloom creates shared creative histories. There is no project token, reward, royalty, public mint, price, or official marketplace, and nothing here is for sale.

What does it actually cost?

A Bitcoin network fee, paid to miners, the same as any other transaction. Each path also holds a small amount of bitcoin while it is alive, and that amount comes back to you when the path ends.

What makes a contribution shared?

Bitcoin confirmation gives everyone the same public ordering, while each path keeps its own identity.

Can two galleries show the same world differently?

Yes. The history is shared. Visual, musical, and social interpretation stays open, and two very different views can both be faithful.

What should I check before taking part?

Read the creative prompt, then read the complete Bitcoin transaction and its network fee in your wallet before you sign anything.
