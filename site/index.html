Questions worth asking

Is ChainBloom a token or trading market?

No. ChainBloom creates shared creative histories, not a project token, reward, royalty, public mint, price, or official marketplace.

What makes a contribution shared?

Bitcoin confirmation gives everyone the same public ordering, while each path keeps its own identity.

Can two galleries show the same world differently?

Yes. The history is shared; visual, musical, and social interpretation remains open.

What should I check before taking part?

Read the creative prompt and review the complete Bitcoin transaction, including its network fee, before signing.
