# Join the ChainBloom community

ChainBloom grows through creative worlds, thoughtful feedback, accessible
experiences, translations, art, education, and community care.

Share an idea by explaining who it helps and how it improves the experience.
When reporting a problem, describe what you saw, what you expected, and any
non-secret details that make the issue easier to understand.

Never include seed phrases, private keys, or unrelated personal information in
a public post. Send security concerns through the private path in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

Community contributions should be respectful, original or properly licensed,
and clear about whether they are official project work or independent community
expression.
